Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about CloudFlow and our demo process.

What's included in the demo?

Our demo is a 30-45 minute personalized walkthrough of CloudFlow's key features tailored to your specific business needs. We'll show you real-world examples of how the platform can solve your unique challenges and answer any questions you have.

Who should attend the demo?

We recommend including key stakeholders who are involved in workflow management, operations, or process improvement in your organization. This often includes operations managers, IT leaders, and department heads who deal with repetitive processes.

Is there any commitment required?

No, there's absolutely no commitment required for a demo. It's simply an opportunity for you to see the platform in action and determine if it's a good fit for your needs.

How soon can we start using CloudFlow after the demo?

If you decide to move forward, most teams can be up and running within 24 hours. We offer quick-start onboarding sessions and extensive documentation to help you get started immediately.

What support is available after implementation?

All CloudFlow customers receive 24/7 email support, live chat during business hours, and access to our comprehensive knowledge base. Higher-tier plans include dedicated account managers and priority phone support.
